## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

To provide the features of Chevvy, we collect personal data and user-generated content. The information collected is categorized as follows:

### A. Account Information
* **Registration Data:** When you register for Chevvy, we collect your name, email address, and a securely encrypted password representation. 
* **Security Credentials:** We use industry-standard hashing algorithms (e.g., bcrypt) to secure your password. Plaintext passwords are never stored on our servers.
* **Session and Access Tokens:** We collect token identifiers to maintain secure, authenticated sessions across your devices.

### B. Spotify Account Information (OAuth Connection)
* **Authentication Tokens:** When you log in via Spotify OAuth, we collect and store secure access tokens and refresh tokens.
* **User Profile Data:** Your Spotify user ID, profile name, image, and email address.
* **Music Selection and Library:** Information about your playlists, tracks, playback status, and specific songs selected for alarm or reminder functions.
* **Playback Controls:** Tokens required to initiate playback, skip tracks, and pause music on your active Spotify devices.

### C. Scheduling and Productivity Data
* **Task Information:** Task titles, descriptions, due dates, reminder times, snooze frequency, and priorities.
* **Task Completion History:** Records of completed, pending, snoozed, or rescheduled tasks. We analyze this information to feed our machine learning algorithms for habit learning and productivity recommendations.

### D. Notes and Content
* **Notes:** We store all text, formatting meta, folder structures, favorites, and tags created within our rich text note system.
* **No Length Constraints:** Notes of unlimited length are stored securely under your account.

### E. Voice Recording and Audio Data
* **Microphone Access:** When you use the voice note or voice-to-text features, the Application requests explicit microphone access.
* **Voice-to-Text Transcription:** Temporary audio streams are captured to transcribe speech in **English**, **Telugu**, or **Hindi** into text notes. Audio streams are processed immediately and are not persistently stored unless you save them as voice notes.

### F. Notification and Device Settings
* **Notification Tokens:** Device tokens required to push local, scheduled, and alarm notifications to your device.
* **Device Settings:** Language choices, theme preferences (including accessibility modes), start-of-day/end-of-day times, and notification preferences.

### G. Usage Analytics and Telemetry
* **Interaction Logs:** Features accessed, button clicks, time spent on tabs, system performance, error logs, and loading durations.

## 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the collected information for the following business and operational purposes:
1. **Service Delivery:** Providing scheduling, notes, alarms, voice transcription, and Spotify syncing.
2. **Habit and Productivity Learning:** Analyzing completion patterns to suggest optimal time blocks (e.g., "We notice you finish Tier-1 tasks faster in the morning. Suggest scheduling next time at 9:00 AM").
3. **Reminders & Alarms:** Using local and scheduled notification services to trigger reminders and play selected music.
4. **Platform Optimization:** Improving design aesthetics, fixing bugs, and improving transcription accuracy.
